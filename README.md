## Hi, I'm Panchali 👋

**I take AI from the first business case through to build and adoption.** I work between business stakeholders and engineering teams on Generative AI, RAG and agentic solutions, and I'm most useful in the stretch where a promising pilot has to become something people actually use.

### What I do

- **Discovery and requirements:** I run discovery with stakeholders across countries, map how work happens today, and write functional specifications for LLM and RAG workflows, including what data they can use and who can see what.
- **Data readiness:** I assess the quality of the data a solution depends on before it's built, since that's where many AI projects quietly fail.
- **Prioritisation and investment cases:** I build the use-case prioritisation framework, roadmap and build-vs-buy cost model that leaders use to decide where to invest.
- **Delivery with engineering:** I set feature priorities, manage the backlog and keep business and engineering aligned on scope through the build.

### Why I build

AI is everywhere now, and almost everyone is using it. The harder part is understanding how an AI product actually works, layer by layer: the model, the data it draws on, the tools it calls, the guardrails around it, and how you test whether it's any good. I found I couldn't get that from reading alone.

So I decided to get hands-on across the layers, on different platforms and tools, by building small, real AI tools end to end. Each project uses a different stack, from coded apps on Amazon Bedrock and Gemini, to managed RAG on Bedrock Knowledge Bases, to low-code agents on Copilot Studio and Power Automate, and I build them with AI-assisted coding using GitHub Copilot and Claude.

Along the way I choose the models, design the guardrails, test against real cases and fix what breaks. It makes me a better partner to engineering teams, and every project here is documented so you can see the decisions behind it.

### 💡 How I work

- **Problem first:** users, requirements and success measures before any build.
- **Rules where it matters, AI where it helps:** deterministic logic for facts and decisions that must be right; the model for language and judgement.
- **Evaluation built in:** test sets and scored evaluation runs. On the payment chaser, a prompt revision raised correct tool use from 87.5% to 95.8% and removed invented content.
- **Guardrails by design:** least-privilege tools, fact checks, prompt-injection defences and a human approval step before anything acts.
- **Decisions on record:** why I chose one model, tool or architecture over another, and what it cost.
